**Banikh** (বণিক, the Bangla word for *merchant*, also romanized Bonik or Banik) lets any Bangladeshi business owner launch a full online store in under 30 minutes, no developer needed.

Everything a local seller needs is built in:

- 💵 **Cash on delivery** with phone-OTP verification
- 📱 **bKash & Nagad** checkout, plus card and bank payments through local aggregators
- 🚚 **Steadfast, Pathao & RedX** couriers with one-click booking and live tracking
- 🏪 **7 storefront templates**, bilingual Bangla/English, custom domains with managed SSL
- 📈 Cart recovery, RFM segments, A/B testing, product feeds, privacy-friendly analytics

**No monthly fee.** Pay 1.5% per delivered order (first 25 orders free, and the fee is free during our launch promotion).
